Giving, in Her Memory

Annie's Angels was created in loving memory of Annie Mah and the kindness she shared with her community. Inspired by her tradition of opening her doors to those in need at Christmas, we hope to continue her legacy by bringing people together through food, gifts and meaningful acts of kindness.

How We Give

Three ways to carry kindness forward.

Christmas Community Lunch

A welcoming meal where everyone is invited to share food, connection and the joy of Christmas.

Toy & Gift Drive

Collecting new toys, books and gifts to help make the holidays brighter for children and families.

Children's Hospital Giving

Sharing comfort and joy with children in hospital through thoughtful gifts, books and approved volunteer activities.

A Legacy That Lives On

To us, giving is about more than gifts. It is about making people feel remembered, welcomed and cared for.

Through Annie's Angels, we hope to keep that spirit alive throughout the year, with a special focus on the Christmas season.
