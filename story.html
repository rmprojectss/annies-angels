Our Story

IN LOVING MEMORY OF ANNIE MAH

Remembering Annie

Annie Mah was someone whose kindness reached beyond her own family and into her community. Her generosity, warmth and willingness to help others left a lasting impression on the people around her.

Each Christmas, Annie continued a special tradition in honour of her late husband, Constable Henry. She opened the doors of a local church and welcomed people to share a Christmas meal, regardless of their circumstances.

A Tradition of Giving

Annie did more than prepare a meal. She brought people together. With the help of volunteers, she organized food, prepared goodie bags filled with everyday essentials, and wrapped toys and gifts for children and families.

She even made the day special with a little excitement, using a microphone to draw names for a few larger gifts. It was about creating a moment of happiness and making everyone feel included.

Continuing Her Legacy

After Annie's passing from pancreatic cancer, the desire to carry forward her tradition became the inspiration behind Annie's Angels.

Through community meals, care packages, toy and gift drives, and support for children in hospital, we hope to continue sharing the compassion that Annie demonstrated throughout her life.

A legacy of kindness does not end with one person. It lives on through every person who chooses to give.

Annie's Angels is created in loving memory of Annie Mah, with gratitude for the example she left behind.
