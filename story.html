Our Story

A Life of Love, Faith and Giving.

Annie Mah was a woman whose kindness, generosity and faith touched everyone around her. She believed in caring for others, especially those in her community who needed a little extra support.

Each Christmas, Annie continued a special tradition in honour of her late husband, Constable Henry. She opened the doors of a local church and welcomed people to share a warm meal, prepared care bags and gifts, and brought joy to families in need.

With the help of volunteers, she made sure people had more than a meal. She wanted everyone to feel welcomed, remembered and cared for.

After Annie's passing from pancreatic cancer, the desire to carry forward her legacy became the inspiration behind Annie's Angels.

Through community meals, care packages, toy and gift drives, and support for children in hospital, we hope to continue sharing the compassion Annie demonstrated throughout her life.

A legacy of kindness does not end with one person. It lives on through every person who chooses to give.

Her Legacy Lives On
