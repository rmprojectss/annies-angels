Making a Difference Through Awareness

Annie's Angels was created in loving memory of Annie Mah. Her journey with pancreatic cancer is an important part of our story and inspires us to help raise awareness, share resources and support those affected by the disease.

Featured Organization

Craig's Cause Pancreatic Cancer Society

Craig's Cause Pancreatic Cancer Society is a Canadian organization dedicated to pancreatic cancer awareness, education, patient support and research.

We encourage our community to explore their resources, learn more about pancreatic cancer and discover ways to get involved in raising awareness.
